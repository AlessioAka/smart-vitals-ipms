**You write the report.** Each section lists where the evidence lives. A weak report copies these notes;
a strong one argues from them, in your own words, with citations.

10. **Evaluation and limitations (LO4)**: honest reflection, what you would do next.
11. **Conclusion**.
12. **References (Harvard)** and **AI-use statement**.
